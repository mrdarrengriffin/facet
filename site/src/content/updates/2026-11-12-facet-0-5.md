Arrange your panel from a browser: open its address to drag, resize and add cards on a live copy of it, with your own
data. Music shows its album art on the Media screen, its Home card and the idle screen, and the new Overhead screen
puts the aircraft over your home on a radar. Home cards come in four sizes, every built-in screen is always there, and
the 480 panel has far more free memory. Update from Home Assistant on either panel; your settings and Home are kept.
