---
title: "Docs: component previews sit in the page"
date: "2026-10-26"
time: "13:30"
kind: "Improved"
---

Component pages now show the live component straight in the page, without the preview bar on top. The preview
follows the site's light or dark theme, picks the 720 or 480 panel size that fits your screen, and stays silent.
Tapping the component still works, and the props controls beside it still change it live.
