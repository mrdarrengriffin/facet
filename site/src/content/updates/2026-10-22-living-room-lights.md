The panel now talks to Home Assistant. A Living room screen lists the room's lights with their state and brightness: tap a row to switch a light, press and hold it to open the Light screen, where the dial sets the brightness. Changes from anywhere (a wall switch, an automation, the app) move the controls on the panel the same way a tap does, and dragging the dial changes the light as you go without flooding Home Assistant with calls. The workbench preview shows the same live values.
