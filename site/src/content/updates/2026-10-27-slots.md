Every place that holds other components now says which components it takes and how many. The header's end slot takes
up to four buttons, status chips or a segmented control, counting its own settings button; a grid cell takes one
component from its list. A screen that breaks a rule is refused when it loads, with the exact place named, instead of
showing a broken layout. The Grid is rebuilt on the new component rules too: it sizes its cells from its own width,
follows token changes live and keeps its repeating items, row limit with a "+N" cell and empty message.
