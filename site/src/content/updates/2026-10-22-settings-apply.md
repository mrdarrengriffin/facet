---
title: "Settings that stick"
date: "2026-10-22"
time: "20:40"
area: "Panels"
---
The Settings screen now changes the panel. Pick a theme or an accent and the screen redraws in it as soon as the control has slid across. Every setting is also a Home Assistant entity in the panel's configuration, saved on the panel, so it survives a restart and automations can change it. The screen, Home Assistant and the workbench all go through one path, so they can't disagree. The panel can also hand its settings over as a small backup file.
