Settings is the first whole screen built only from Facet components: the header, section headers, list rows, segmented controls, accent swatches, switches, sliders and a bottom sheet for longer lists. Every personalisation setting comes from one settings list, grouped into Look, Sound, Clock and idle, Behaviour and Units, so the panel, Home Assistant and these docs can't drift apart.

A changed value moves into place with its own animation instead of the screen being drawn again: switches slide, sliders sweep with their number following, a picked option shows in its row, and the screen keeps its scroll position.
