Every screen now ends its header with the time in the top right corner, so the clock never moves. It is a layout rule, not something each screen draws. On Home a small settings button sits just to its left, and its icon keeps its accent knob in every state, exactly as on the Icons page.

The group names on Settings (Look, Sound and the rest) now use the same label size as the screen header, as on the original docs, instead of a smaller caption.
