The last of the components from the first Facet are now part of the new renderer.

- **Lightning radar** puts strikes on rings at 10, 25, 50 and 100 km, on a square-root scale so the near rings get room. Each strike is coloured by its age, and the nearest one in the last 15 minutes gets a ring. A new strike sends out a ripple that grows and fades. On its page, a sample storm plays at 20x speed, so strikes arrive and age while you watch.
- **3D print model** draws the print as an isometric voxel model. The printed part is solid, the last few layers are in the accent colour, and the part still to print is a faint ghost with hairline edges.
- **Card previews** are the small pictures at the top right of a Home card: agents, a record, a dial, a switch, a clock, a timer, a branch, energy bars and a mini radar. Each one is built from the same parts as the full components.

State readout, Moon facts (the phase, moonrise and moonset, worked out on the panel with no service), a scroll track for long lists, a mascot mood sample and a Units page also landed. Units is one setting each for distance and speed, and every screen follows it.
