title: "Tidier panel in Home Assistant"
date: "2026-10-23"
time: "18:30"
kind: "Improved"
---
A Facet panel now shows up in Home Assistant with a short, readable list: a Screen picker that opens any page of the
deck (handy for "show Cameras when the doorbell rings"), Wi-Fi signal, and a Firmware entry that offers new releases and
installs only when you press Install. Settings the panel doesn't act on yet stay hidden until they do.
