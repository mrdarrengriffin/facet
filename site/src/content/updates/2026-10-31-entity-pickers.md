A screen's entity settings now pick from the panel's own Home Assistant list. Opening a picker reads only that
picker's kind of entity, shows a loading row meanwhile and keeps the list until Read now. Long lists narrow by letter
with a tap, no keyboard needed. Without a link, the picker says so and opens Settings > Home Assistant.
