A theme file now restyles every screen built on the new renderer while it runs: cards, buttons, the dial's handle and
scale, the header and the page frame take the theme's colours, edges, bevels and shadows, with nothing rebuilt. Two
themes ship beside Facet, Blockcraft and Win98, and a theme that would make text hard to read or hide a button press is
refused before it reaches the screen.

Themes bring their own type too: Blockcraft draws in Monocraft and Win98 in W95FA (open-licence pixel fonts), without
moving a line. Pixel themes draw arcs, scale marks, the handle and icons in whole pixels of the theme's grid, with no
smoothing, so they look crisp rather than blurred.
