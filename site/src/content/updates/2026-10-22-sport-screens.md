A first set of live sport screens. Every circuit of the 2026 Formula 1 season is drawn from survey data in Facet's own line (about 8 KB for all 23), with the cars gliding round it and the running order beside it; tennis puts the point in play first, with the court showing who serves into which box; football shows the score and minute over both line-ups in formation and the match's events.

Each screen reads one simple shape of data, so any source can feed it, and each comes with a repeatable simulator that plays a whole race, match or game by the real rules.
