The Energy screen no longer asks which sensors to use. Home Assistant already knows: the panel reads its Energy
dashboard (the grid, solar, battery and their cost) and the statistics behind it, so today's half-hours, the
cost so far and the last two weeks just appear. Settings > Screens > Energy says what it found there, or that the
Energy dashboard isn't set up yet.
