Facet can now make the floor plan for you from the robot vacuum's own map: every room it knows, straightened into
clean walls, with the robot landing in the right room. It reads Roborock-style map pictures, Valetudo maps and room
outlines, and it tells you what it trimmed (like floor that leaked out through a patio door) instead of hiding it.
