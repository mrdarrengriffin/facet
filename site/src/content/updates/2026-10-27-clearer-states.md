What shows a state now stands out at least 3:1 from everything round it, in every theme and mode: the chosen segment
gets a firmer outline, the Slider's fill and handle sit on a quieter track in the accent's deeper shade, and the Win98
handle has a black outline so it never melts into the grey window. Disabled controls stay visible but quieter.

In the Win98 theme the Toggle is now the classic checkbox: a white sunken square with a black tick when on, the same
size to tap as before.
