A speaker picked for Media, in Settings or on the screen's speaker sheet, now stays picked. Auto is a choice of its own in both lists: only then does the screen follow what's playing in the house.

The cassette visual sits where the disc does, its right edge on the disc's slot, and the rest runs off the screen's left edge, as in the original. CD and vinyl fit the slot whole.
