Phosphor joins the built-in themes: a green-screen terminal look with near-black glass, phosphor-green text and
square edges. Themes can also be sent to a panel from Home Assistant as a small file, no new firmware needed.
Dragging a dial on the panels is about three times smoother, and a moving knob no longer leaves marks behind in
themes that draw it with an outline.
