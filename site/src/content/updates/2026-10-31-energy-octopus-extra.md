Energy now takes one optional extra on top of Home Assistant's Energy dashboard, and the first is the Octopus Energy
integration. If the dashboard's grid meter comes from it, the panel finds the rest by itself: the live watts and the
rate now and next in the header ("536 W · 43.2p now → 28.8p at 19:00"), today's cheap and peak half-hours on the
rate strip with the cheapest stretch still to come, and the next saving session, power-down or greener night. The
Home card shows the watts and the price, coloured when it's cheap or peak. Settings > Screens > Energy > Extra data
picks Auto, Octopus or None, and a test-data source lets anyone try an extra without owning it.
