The rest of the controls now run on the panel renderer: the arc and tick ring, the thermostat dial, the window drawing for blinds and curtains, the checkbox, and the media visuals (cassette, CD, and vinyl with its tonearm). They turn at their real speeds, and the tape or arm follows the track. Every round control now shares one set of rules for its scale, arc and "where is the finger", so the light dial and the thermostat feel the same.
