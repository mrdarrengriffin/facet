The groundwork every chart shares: series colours that pass contrast in both themes, line weights for each panel, bars, a gauge, labels, and what a chart shows with no data, a missing sensor or gaps. Press and drag across a chart to read it. Chart motion has its own section on the Motion page: lines draw in, new readings slide in and the latest point sends out a small ping.

Later the same day: when a new reading arrives, the end dot now moves up or down to it while the whole line slides left by one step, together, so the dot looks like it draws the line as it goes; the ping starts where the dot lands. This now plays on the 480 panel too. Scrubbing got a voice: the ticks rise and fall with the line under your finger, higher readings ticking higher, within a gentle part of the sound pack's pitch range.
