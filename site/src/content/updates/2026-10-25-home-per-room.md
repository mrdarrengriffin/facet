Each room gets its own Home: tiles that toggle a light with one tap, cards that open a screen, wide cards for the big
things, and quiet readouts for sensors that just report. A kitchen Home can swap the settings button for a timer
shortcut and show the time left beside the clock. Arrange it all in the editor: click a card to change what it shows,
its size or what a tap does.
