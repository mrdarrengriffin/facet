---
title: "Navigation: Home is always one step away"
date: "2026-10-26"
time: "13:00"
kind: "Improved"
---

Getting around the panel now follows a simple hierarchy with Home at the root. Every screen except Home has a Back
button (a screen can't hide it), Back goes up one level, and holding Back goes straight Home. You are never more than
two screens away from Home. A screen sent by an agent or by AI opens one level above Home and replaces the last sent
screen, so they never stack up, and sending or updating a screen no longer takes over the display unless the sender
asks to show it. Tiles in a card layout can now open a screen when tapped.
