The screen headers now carry what the original ones had on the right: the speaker button on Media, the window picker on Blinds and curtains, the live power on Energy and the place name on Weather.

Media can also show the cassette, CD or vinyl in place of the disc, and the Thermostat's minus button and mode buttons are fixed on the big panel.
