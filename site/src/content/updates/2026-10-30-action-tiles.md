A Home tile can now show any Home Assistant entity, and what a tap does follows the kind of thing it is: a light
toggles or opens its screen, an automation turns on and off or runs now, a script runs, a scene activates, a button
presses, a sensor just shows its value. Turn on "Ask first" and the panel checks "Run Good night?" before it does
anything. Buttons you put on your own screens can do the same.
