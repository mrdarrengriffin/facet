The tennis screen can now follow a real match. The button in its header opens a sheet with today's tournaments and how many matches are live in each; a tournament lists its matches under Live, Upcoming and Results, and picking one moves the screen onto it. The point, the games and who serves update by themselves, with the same lift as a tap. An upcoming match shows when it starts and turns live in place.

The data usually runs ahead of the TV. Sync with TV puts a slim bar under the score: pause when a point lands on the screen, play when the TV shows it, and the screen stays that far behind from then on. Small steps fine-tune it.

The football screen has the same picker. With nothing picked, both screens keep running their simulators.
