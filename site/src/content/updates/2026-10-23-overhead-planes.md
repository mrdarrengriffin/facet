The Overhead screen is back to its original design: every aircraft on the sky radar is a little plane pointing where
it is flying, the selected one ringed, with N / E / S / W round the edge. The closest aircraft leads until you tap
another. A Now / Radar switch in the header opens the radar full size, with ring distances and callsigns. The rows
show the type, height and distance above each route, and distances and speeds follow your units.
