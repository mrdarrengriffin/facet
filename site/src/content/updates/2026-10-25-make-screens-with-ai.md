Ask an AI assistant for any screen you like and it comes out looking like Facet: it writes a small spec from the
released components, the panel checks it, and the design system does the rest. A new guide shows how, with twenty live
example screens (bin day, trains, game progress, AI agents, shopping list and more), each with its spec and prompt.
