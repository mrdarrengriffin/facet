List rows, section headers and empty states now take the width of whatever holds them, so they line up in a sheet,
a card or a narrow column as well as on a full page. A list row's right end is now a slot: next to its value, chevron
or switch, a screen can add one more component, such as a status chip or a count. All three follow token changes live.
