title: "Panels now have pages"
date: "2026-10-23"
time: "17:45"
kind: "New feature"
---
Each panel now keeps its own list of pages in your order, starting from a Home that will hold your cards. Screens
for one thing, like a light or a blind, open from what you tap instead of crowding the list, and Settings stays
part of the panel.
