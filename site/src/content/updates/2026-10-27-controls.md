Toggles, segmented controls, sliders, buttons and count badges now take every colour and size from the shared tokens,
so switching between light and dark repaints them at once; a segmented control no longer keeps its old colours after a
theme change. A slider now fills the width it is given, and a button can stretch across its space with the words
centred. They look and move as before.
