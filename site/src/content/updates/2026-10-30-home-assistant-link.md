Settings > Home Assistant holds the panel's own link: Home Assistant's address and a long-lived token, kept on the
panel and never shown back. Read now fetches the entities the screens' pickers offer, straight from Home Assistant,
reading the reply as it streams so even a large home fits in a little memory.
