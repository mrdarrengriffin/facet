Settings now has a Screens page: every screen with where its data comes from and a switch to take it off Home. Each screen has its own page for its source and options, such as the cassette, CD or vinyl on Media.

The Media screen's progress ring now follows the track as it plays, and the Blinds screen moves the real blind.
