Settings > Text size now has a working Large option. Titles, body text and small labels each go one step bigger inside the same line boxes, so nothing on a screen moves or reflows. The panel switches straight away and keeps the choice after a restart, and Home Assistant can change it too. On the smaller 480 px panel only titles grow: the other lines there have no room to spare. Every component and screen was checked in Large text at both sizes.
