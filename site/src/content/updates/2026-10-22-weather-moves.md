The weather icons animate on the panels as they did on the old site: the sun's rays turn slowly, clouds drift, rain, snow and hail fall, lightning flashes and stars twinkle. Each icon is the same drawing cut into its parts, so with reduced motion it rests as the static icon, and the timings live with the other Motion tokens. The moon phase glyph is drawn on the panel for any night of the month, and follows a slider on the Icons page as you drag.
