Home cards get a cleaner look, the editor lets you drag cards into place, and every built-in screen is now simply there: no more switching screens on and off. The Overhead flight radar shows where each flight is going, and album art that used to show a plain drawing now appears.

## New

- **Drag cards to move them in the editor.** Open your panel's address in a browser and drag a card to a new place (on touch, long press it first). A card that opens a screen also shows that screen's look in its panel (Media's cassette is one click away), with a link to all its settings. #195 #189 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/editor/)
- **A State readout card for Home.** A sensor's value as a half-size card, added from the editor's Add card. #199 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/docs/home-cards/)
- **Overhead shows where each flight is going.** Every flight now shows from → to, looked up by its callsign, whichever source the radar uses (before, only the FlightRadar24 source had routes). #157 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/screens/overhead/)
- **Helicopters and military jets on the radar.** They get their own shapes on the Overhead radar. #160 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/screens/overhead/)
- **Tap Update to check now.** In Settings > Update, a tap checks for an update straight away and shows the result, instead of waiting for the hourly check. #159 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/help/troubleshooting/)

## Improved

- **Built-in screens are always there.** Clock, Weather, Calendar, Media, Energy, Moon, Overhead and Timer no longer need switching on. Settings > Screens lists every one; add a screen's card to Home like any other card. A screen's data (for example the Overhead radar's flight feed) only runs while the screen shows or a Home card can open it. #177 #198 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/editor/)
- **New Home card heads.** Small and half-size cards put the name top-left and the icon top-right, with the state at the bottom; a long name takes two lines. Half cards line up evenly. #192 #193 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/docs/home-cards/)
- **Media cards show the album art.** A media player's card on Home prints the album art on its record, and the record turns while it plays. The editor shows the same. #196 #201 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/docs/home-cards/)
- **Easier picking in the editor.** Add card lists every entity of the card's type before you type a search. #190 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/editor/)
- **Energy reads more clearly.** The price now and next show as values, a saving session as its own chip, and the price's word when its entity is missing. #162 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/screens/energy/)
- **Overhead settings say where the radar is centred** in the Source row, instead of a separate row. #155 (https://mrdarrengriffin.github.io/facet/screens/overhead/)

## Fixed

- **Album art showed a plain drawing.** Progressive JPEG covers (common for radio and TV artwork) now decode on both panels. #154
- **The idle screen's album art fell back to a small picture or none.** PNG covers fit again, and the idle art no longer runs out of room. #186
- **The Light screen lost its On / Off word.** Lights and switches that only turn on and off show On / Off on the dial again. #171
- **Home could show a Back button that went nowhere.** The start Home never shows Back, and opening Home from Home Assistant or an agent opens it properly. #202

## After updating

- **Screens you had switched off are back in the list.** They are listed in Settings > Screens with the others. Your deck, its cards and each screen's settings are kept; remove a card you don't want from Home in the editor.

## Known issues

- **Idle album art can still disappear on the Guition.** It can go after a while. A fix is in this release but not yet confirmed; it is tracked for the next beta. #197
