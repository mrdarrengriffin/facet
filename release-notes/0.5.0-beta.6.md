Album art comes to Facet: the Media screen's CD and vinyl show the cover of what's playing, and the record turns with it, there's a new plain square Art visual, and the idle screen can show the cover full screen while music plays. Also new: a nightly Dev update channel, and Home Assistant's update dialog now lists everything since your version.

## New

- **Album art on the Media screen.** The CD and vinyl show the cover of what's playing, on the Media screen and its Home card. While the art loads, or when there is none, you see the usual drawing. Needs the panel's own Home Assistant link (Settings > Home Assistant). #100 #91
- **Album art on the idle screen.** Set Idle shows to Album art and, while music plays, the idle screen is the cover, full screen. When the music is paused it goes back to the clock; a tap still goes Home. #101
- **A Dev update channel for trying fixes early.** Set Update channel to Dev and the panel is offered a build of the latest code each night something changes. Dev builds are tested less than betas, so they can break things: switch back to Beta or Stable whenever you like. Your channel choice is kept, and a panel is never offered an older build than the one it runs. #102 #110 #111
- **The record turns with its cover.** On the Vinyl visual the whole record, cover label included, turns while music plays and slows to a stop where it is when you pause. #115
- **The CD turns with its cover too.** Like the record, the CD and its cover turn while music plays and slow to a stop where they are when you pause. To keep the cover still, turn off Spin during playback in Settings > Screens > Media: a small mark then shows the CD turning. On the Guition the turning cover is drawn a little less smoothly while it moves, to keep it light on the processor; it is sharp again the moment it stops. #115 #119
- **A square album-art picture for the Media screen.** Set the Media screen's Visual to Art and it shows the album art of what is playing as a plain square, nothing turning; with no art it shows a plain square. #108

## Improved

- **Disc is now CD.** The plain Disc visual has been folded into the CD, which is now the default Media visual. A Media screen set to Disc shows the CD; nothing to do. #115
- **Sharper album art.** Every cover is now drawn at the size it is shown on that panel: the Guition's full-screen idle cover is a full 480 x 480 instead of 320 px blown up (which looked pixelated), and the Waveshare's is 720 x 720. Covers smaller than the screen are scaled up smoothly. If the Guition is short of memory it falls back to a smaller, smoothly scaled cover rather than none. #117
- **"Clock after" is now "Idle after".** The idle timeout setting is named for what it does now that the idle screen can show album art, on the panel and in Home Assistant. Your setting is kept. #116 #122
- **Album art changes with every track.** The next track's cover now always loads, also on the Guition, and the cover you see stays until the new one has fully arrived, then swaps. A download that breaks off or a picture that arrives cut short is never shown half-loaded: you keep the usual drawing instead. #109
- **No more "Entity lists / Read now" in Settings > Home Assistant.** The panel never needed to read every entity at once: each entity picker reads just its own kind of entity when you open it, and if its list is more than five minutes old it refreshes it in the background while showing the one it has, so entities you add in Home Assistant turn up on their own. Screens still follow only the entities they show. #120
- **The update lists everything since your version.** When an update is offered, the dialog shows the notes of every version between the one on your panel and the new one, each under its own heading, so nothing you skipped goes unseen. #106

## Fixed

- **Calendar settings take effect at once.** A change in Settings > Screens > Calendar now shows straight away on the Calendar screen and its Home card, with no restart. #98
- **The update card no longer sticks on Installing.** After an update, Home Assistant shows the panel up to date the moment the new firmware is confirmed, about 90 seconds after it restarts, instead of staying on Installing. If the panel could not check for updates straight away, it tries again every minute. #55

- **No flicker on play / pause.** Playing or pausing no longer redraws the Media screen or its Home card: the cover stays put and isn't downloaded again. #113 #114
- **No more flickering band on the Guition.** A band of the picture could flicker for a moment, mostly while album art loaded or after a settings change. The panel no longer starves the screen while it works out where a picture fits. #118
- **Safer updates.** A new firmware is kept only once it has run properly (up 90 seconds with Wi-Fi and Home Assistant connected). If it can't get there in three tries, the panel goes back to the firmware it had. #118

## After updating

- The Idle after setting is a new entity in Home Assistant; remove the old unavailable Clock after one and update any automations that used it. #122

## Known issues

- **A Guition's Restart reason can read "watchdog" after a normal restart.** The panel restarts fine and updates are not affected; only the reason shown is wrong. Seen in beta.5 too. #121

- **A Guition panel once restarted on tapping Allow for a new API key straight after Revoke all.** Not reproduced since; the key is still saved. #92
