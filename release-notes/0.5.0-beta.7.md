## New

- **Overhead: the aircraft over your home, on a radar (preview in the docs).** A new screen shows the flights round your home: where to look for the closest one, its route, height and speed, a radar with their trails, a list of all of them and each one's details, and a Home card. It reads the open adsb.lol data with no account or key, or the FlightRadar24 integration or your own ADS-B receiver in Home Assistant, with a range and a lowest altitude to choose. Try it on the docs site now; it comes to the panels in a later beta. #124

## Fixed

- **Idle album art no longer comes out with shifted rows.** After the Guition had been running a while, a new track's cover on the idle screen could come in with every other row shifted sideways, looking blocky and interlaced. Covers the panel shrinks while decoding (also the Media screen's CD art) are now decoded properly, and the idle screen keeps memory set aside for its full-screen cover, so it no longer has to fall back to a smaller one. #127
- **Energy costs no longer flick to £0.00.** On the Energy screen (and its Home card), today's cost could drop to £0.00 for a moment every minute or so while the panel re-read the day from Home Assistant, most often with a live power sensor that updates every few seconds. The last good figures now stay up until the new read has landed. #62
- **Screenshots work again on the Guition.** Taking a screenshot of the panel (its `/screenshot` address) failed once the idle screen had set memory aside for album art. The panel now sends the picture straight from the screen a few rows at a time, without copying the whole screen first, and holds the drawing still while it does, so the picture never comes out half old, half new. #131
- **Text stays inside its box in the Blockcraft and Windows 98 themes.** The Moon screen's phase line, the word Unavailable in place of a switch or a segmented control, the compact timer ring and a big number with a unit no longer spill out of their boxes with these themes' wider fonts; a line that is too long now ends in "...". #81
- **A quick tap while a page is getting ready is no longer lost.** The panel prepares pages in the background; a tap that started and ended while one was being built did nothing. The panel now keeps checking the touch screen during the build, even inside the bigger screens like Moon, Media and Calendar, and acts on the tap as soon as the build is done. #94
- **One missed read no longer blanks the Energy screen.** If a single read from Home Assistant failed, the Energy screen switched straight to "Home Assistant not reached" and £--. It now keeps the last figures and only says Home Assistant is not reached after two failed reads in a row, or three minutes without a read. #132
- **Energy cost follows your tariff through the day.** When the Energy dashboard has a price but no cost sensor of its own, the panel worked out the cost as today's kWh times the price right now, which was wrong on time-of-use tariffs and jumped at every rate change. It now uses the cost Home Assistant itself works out (each moment at the price of that moment), the same figure as Home Assistant's Energy dashboard. If Home Assistant has no such cost, the panel shows its estimate marked with "~". #134
