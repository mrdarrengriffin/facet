Album art on the idle screen stays sharp all day.

## New

- **Overhead: the aircraft over your home, on a radar.** A new screen shows the flights round your home: where to look for the closest one, its route, height and speed, a radar with their trails, a list of all of them and each one's details, and a Home card. By default the panel reads the open adsb.lol data itself, with no account or key (it goes to the internet every 10 s while the screen or its card shows); it can also use the FlightRadar24 integration or your own ADS-B receiver in Home Assistant. Choose a range and a lowest altitude in its settings. #124 #146

- **Report a problem straight from the panel.** Settings > Report a problem shows a QR code that opens a new bug report on GitHub with your panel's version and board filled in, and the address of the panel's own problem report (`http://<panel>/fw/report`). The report keeps the log from before a restart, every Wi-Fi and Home Assistant drop with how long it lasted and why, the reason for each of the last few restarts and, after a crash, where it crashed, even after the panel has been unplugged. Tokens, keys and passwords are taken out before it leaves the panel. #125

- **Reset pairing when Home Assistant says the encryption key is invalid.** If the panel holds an encryption key Home Assistant no longer has (Home Assistant restored from an older backup, or the panel added to another Home Assistant), Home Assistant could not connect and the only fixes were an old backup or a reflash over USB. Settings > Home Assistant now shows the panel's encryption key and **Reset pairing** (hold to confirm), which clears it; Home Assistant then sets the panel up again with a new key, keeping its device and entities. It also works without touching the panel: `POST http://<panel>/fw/pairing/reset` with an API key. #150

- **Each panel has its own update password.** Until now anyone on your network could flash firmware onto a panel over Wi-Fi. A panel now makes its own password the first time it starts on this version, and network flashes (ESPHome's `esphome upload`, `espota`) need it. Updates from Home Assistant's Firmware entity and USB flashing are unchanged. If you flash your own builds over Wi-Fi, find the password under Settings > Update password (hold the row) or with the Home Assistant action `<panel>_facet_update_password`. #149

## Fixed

- **Idle album art no longer comes out with shifted rows.** After the Guition had been running a while, a new track's cover on the idle screen could come in with every other row shifted sideways, looking blocky and interlaced. Covers the panel shrinks while decoding (also the Media screen's CD art) are now decoded properly, and the idle screen keeps memory set aside for its full-screen cover, so it no longer has to fall back to a smaller one. #127
- **Energy costs no longer flick to £0.00.** On the Energy screen (and its Home card), today's cost could drop to £0.00 for a moment every minute or so while the panel re-read the day from Home Assistant, most often with a live power sensor that updates every few seconds. The last good figures now stay up until the new read has landed. #62
- **Screenshots work again on the Guition.** Taking a screenshot of the panel (its `/screenshot` address) failed once the idle screen had set memory aside for album art. The panel now sends the picture straight from the screen a few rows at a time, without copying the whole screen first, and holds the drawing still while it does, so the picture never comes out half old, half new. #131
- **Text stays inside its box in the Blockcraft and Windows 98 themes.** The Moon screen's phase line, the word Unavailable in place of a switch or a segmented control, the compact timer ring and a big number with a unit no longer spill out of their boxes with these themes' wider fonts; a line that is too long now ends in "...". #81
- **A quick tap while a page is getting ready is no longer lost.** The panel prepares pages in the background; a tap that started and ended while one was being built did nothing. The panel now keeps checking the touch screen during the build, even inside the bigger screens like Moon, Media and Calendar, and acts on the tap as soon as the build is done. #94
- **One missed read no longer blanks the Energy screen.** If a single read from Home Assistant failed, the Energy screen switched straight to "Home Assistant not reached" and £--. It now keeps the last figures and only says Home Assistant is not reached after two failed reads in a row, or three minutes without a read. #132
- **Energy cost follows your tariff through the day.** When the Energy dashboard has a price but no cost sensor of its own, the panel worked out the cost as today's kWh times the price right now, which was wrong on time-of-use tariffs and jumped at every rate change. It now uses the cost Home Assistant itself works out (each moment at the price of that moment), the same figure as Home Assistant's Energy dashboard. If Home Assistant has no such cost, the panel shows its estimate marked with "~". #134
- **The Energy screen asks Home Assistant for much less.** Each minute it now reads only the last half hour of the day instead of the whole day since yesterday (a full read every 15 minutes keeps it exact), about a tenth of the data late in the day, and the reads that come together share one connection to Home Assistant instead of opening one each. #133
- **Album art on the Waveshare is decoded by the panel's own JPEG chip, and Apple Music covers come at the right size.** The Waveshare now decodes and scales JPEG album art in hardware, about three times faster than before (a typical cover in about 130 ms instead of 400 to 580), falling back to the software decoder for anything the chip can't do (progressive JPEGs, very small pictures); the Guition is unchanged. Apple Music covers are now asked for at the size the panel shows them, so the full-screen idle art on the Waveshare is sharp and small covers download less. The panel's info page shows which decoder ran and how long it took. #130
- **"Check for updates" sees a new version straight away.** Right after a release or a new Dev build, the panel could say it was up to date for up to ten minutes, because the website handed it a saved copy of the update list. Each check, including Home Assistant's check for updates and the release notes the panel reads, now asks for a fresh copy. #148
- **Dev builds reach the panel the same day.** A new build on the Dev channel was sometimes published but not offered to panels until the next change to the website, because the step that puts it on the website failed. It now goes up with every Dev build. #151
- **The Guition restarts cleanly.** Now and then a restart (the Restart button, or the restart after an update) ended in a watchdog reset instead, so Home Assistant showed "watchdog" as the restart reason. A restart now goes straight to the full reset the Guition does anyway, and is a few seconds quicker too. #121
- **The panel no longer restarts while something reads its status.** Reading the panel's status page (`/fw/info`) often, as the editor and scripts do, could overflow the small memory its web server works in and restart the Guition. That page is now put together by the panel's main loop instead. #128
