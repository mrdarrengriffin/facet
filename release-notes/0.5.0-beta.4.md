Media and Calendar now set themselves up from Home Assistant. The Media screen finds your speakers and music sources on its own, Spotify included, and the Calendar screen shows every calendar you have, with a quick on/off for each right on the screen. Fewer settings to fill in, and both screens recover by themselves when Home Assistant comes back after a restart.

## New

- **Media finds your music sources in Home Assistant.** The Source list is built from the integrations you already have: Music Assistant, Spotify, Google Cast and Sonos. Spotify plays through Home Assistant, so your Spotify Connect devices appear as speakers and no separate Spotify sign-in is needed. #6
- **Every speaker on the Media screen.** The speakers sheet lists every speaker of the chosen source: the one playing first, then by room, then by name. The screen follows whichever speaker is playing, with nothing to set up. #7
- **Every calendar on the Calendar screen.** The Calendar shows all of Home Assistant's calendars by default. The calendars sheet turns each one off or on, and your choice is kept across restarts. #8

## Improved

- **Media and Calendar recover after Home Assistant restarts.** If a read fails while Home Assistant is away, the screen tries again every 30 seconds instead of staying on Not found.
- **Cleaner settings sheets.** Theme and Screen-off rows show a single radio mark per row instead of a tick and a radio.

## After updating

- Media's **Player**, **Speakers** and **Auto follows** settings are gone. Pick your source and speaker on the Media screen itself.
- The Calendar's **Calendars** setting is gone. Every calendar shows at first: hide the ones you don't want from the calendars sheet on the screen.

## Known issues

- **The update card can show Installing for about two minutes after the panel restarts.** That is the new firmware settling: don't unplug the panel until it reads up to date. If it stays on Installing longer, tell us on #55.
- **A Guition panel once restarted on tapping Allow for a new API key straight after Revoke all.** Not reproduced since; the key is still saved. #92
